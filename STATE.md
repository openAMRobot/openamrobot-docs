## Goal of the roadmap model

The roadmap becomes a renderer of the expanded combined_robot.xacro (openamr-upperbody-sw) at a pinned commit, with no hand-typed dimensions in model.js.

- One geometry, two presentations.
- URDF only: physics, inertials, collisions, ros2_control, MoveIt, Gazebo.
- Roadmap only: finishes, labels, exploded offsets, BOM classes, harness, fasteners, design-intent parts. Roadmap-only parts always attach to a URDF link with an offset (`scripts/roadmap_model/link_map.yaml`).
- Convergence runs in phases: 1) warn-only parity report (done, 2 Nov 2026: `parity.py`, CI warning); 2) URDF-to-GLB renderer plus an overlay file keyed by URDF link name; 3) the parity check fails CI above 0.5 mm, and the frozen fixture in upperbody-sw is removed; 4) the combined model feeds the lift joint, limits, mass, COM, collision reports and recorded MoveIt trajectories into the roadmap, and the roadmap feeds BOM masses, visual-only detail links and confirmed design-intent datums back (`mass_export.yaml` is the first input).
