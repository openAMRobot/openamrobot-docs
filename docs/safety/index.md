# Safety

OpenAMRobot combines moving machinery, electrical power, batteries, embedded firmware, networks and autonomous software. Public source availability is not a declaration of safety, regulatory compliance or fitness for a particular deployment.

## Minimum integration controls

- perform a documented risk assessment for the complete configured system;
- provide accessible emergency stopping and safe power isolation;
- validate speed, force, payload, stability, braking and stopping distance;
- control charging, battery, thermal, electrical and stored-energy hazards;
- restrict network and operator access;
- test communication loss, sensor failure, software crash and unexpected restart;
- establish a controlled test area before autonomous operation; and
- comply with applicable machinery, radio, electrical, battery, workplace and data-protection requirements.

Component-specific limits, wiring, configuration and warnings belong in the relevant repository and take priority over this overview. Security vulnerabilities must be reported privately under the [Security Policy](https://github.com/openAMRobot/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

OpenAMRobot is experimental. Independent engineering validation and certification may be required before real-world deployment.
