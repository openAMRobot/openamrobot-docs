## Partner principles

1. **Customer value first:** each offer addresses a measurable workflow or capability gap.
2. **Start narrow:** begin with one authorized offer or delivery role.
3. **Open and commercial are distinct:** source licenses do not grant certification or trademark rights.
4. **Quality compounds:** training, deployment and support must follow documented standards.
5. **Evidence unlocks expansion:** territory, scope and rights grow after successful delivery.
