| Service partner | Existing robot owners | Maintenance and local support | Service agreements |
| Technology partner | Developers and startups | Extensions and integrations | Software and engineering fees |

## Selection test

Choose the model for which you can answer **yes** to most of these questions:

- Do we already reach the target customer?
- Can we demonstrate the value credibly?
- Can we deliver and support the result locally?
- Do we understand the safety and quality obligations?
- Can we repeat the offer without founder-only heroics?
- Is there a clear paid next step?

!!! tip "Combine later"
    A training partner can become an integrator; an integrator can add support; a hardware partner can host training. Validate one motion before combining several.
