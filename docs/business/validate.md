# Validate local demand

**For:** entrepreneurs and potential partners deciding whether to invest in a local OpenAMRobot offer.

## Validation funnel

<div class="oamr-path"><span>Qualified conversations</span><b>→</b><span>Discovery calls</span><b>→</b><span>Proposals</span><b>→</b><span>Paid engagement</span><b>→</b><span>Pilot</span></div>

Ask about a real workflow—not general interest in robotics:

| Area | Questions |
| --- | --- |
| Process | What happens, how often, and who owns it? |
| Pain | Labor, delay, error, safety, capacity or consistency? |
| Constraints | Site, payload, people, interfaces and regulation? |
| Economics | Current cost, cost of delay and available budget? |
| Decision | Budget owner, start date and acceptable pilot? |
| Evidence | What metric would justify the next commercial step? |

!!! warning "Weak signals"
    Likes, event attendance and general enthusiasm are useful for awareness but do not validate a business. Prioritize proposal requests, deposits, paid workshops and pilots.
