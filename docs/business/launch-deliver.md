## Minimum launch system

<div class="oamr-grid">
<article class="oamr-card"><h3>Offer</h3><p>Customer, problem, outcome, scope, price, exclusions and next step.</p></article>
<article class="oamr-card"><h3>Sales process</h3><p>Qualification, discovery, proposal, follow-up and handover.</p></article>
<article class="oamr-card"><h3>Delivery process</h3><p>Inputs, preparation, procedure, outputs, quality check and acceptance.</p></article>
<article class="oamr-card"><h3>Technical baseline</h3><p>Approved release, configuration, safety procedure and fallback.</p></article>
<article class="oamr-card"><h3>Support</h3><p>Owner, channel, service level, escalation and spare-parts plan.</p></article>
<article class="oamr-card oamr-card--success"><h3>Evidence</h3><p>Metrics, testimonial, case study, repeat request and commercial progression.</p></article>
</div>

## Recommended first offers

| Offer | Best for | Commercial next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Robotics Opportunity Workshop | Companies with an unclear automation opportunity | Feasibility or pilot proposal |
| Discovery Workshop / Demo Day | Hubs and professional communities | Cohort or corporate workshop |
| Operator & Trainer program | Operations teams and domain experts | Enablement or deployment |
| Productization Sprint | Startups and engineering teams | Engineering project or PoC |
