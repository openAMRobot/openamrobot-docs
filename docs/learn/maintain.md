## Maintenance system

| Area | Examples | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Routine | Daily, weekly and monthly checks | Signed inspection record |
| Diagnosis | State, logs, power, communication, sensors | Isolated fault and evidence |
| Repair | Drive, battery, sensor, cable or connector | Verified replacement or repair |
| Spares | Critical modules and consumables | Site-appropriate stock |
| Updates | Software, firmware, release notes, rollback | Controlled configuration state |

!!! tip "Commercial relevance"
    Maintenance and local support are not an afterthought: they are essential to customer trust and a recurring-service opportunity for qualified partners.
