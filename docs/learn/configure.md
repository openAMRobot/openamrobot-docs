## Configuration model

<div class="oamr-grid">
<article class="oamr-card"><h3>Identity & network</h3><p>Robot name, site, access point, remote access and security basics.</p></article>
<article class="oamr-card"><h3>Hardware inventory</h3><p>Drive, sensors, compute, lift, arms, grippers and device packages.</p></article>
<article class="oamr-card"><h3>Geometry & calibration</h3><p>URDF, joints, limits, meshes, odometry, IMU, lidar and cameras.</p></article>
<article class="oamr-card"><h3>Navigation & docking</h3><p>Footprint, planners, costmaps, maps, localization and approach tuning.</p></article>
<article class="oamr-card oamr-card--accent"><h3>Manipulation</h3><p>Planning groups, named poses, grippers, workspace and lift limits.</p></article>
<article class="oamr-card oamr-card--success"><h3>Safety limits</h3><p>Speed, zones, keep-out areas, emergency behavior and site constraints.</p></article>
</div>
