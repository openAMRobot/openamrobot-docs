> **Domain expertise — not coding — becomes the programming interface.**

Botshare Academy is the professional training and adoption layer of the OpenAMRobot ecosystem. It is built for **domain experts — the people who understand the task but are not robotics engineers**.

Instead of teaching every participant to become a robotics specialist, we teach them to **operate a robot, demonstrate a task, correct its behaviour, evaluate the result, and connect the skill to a measurable business outcome**.

The Certified Training Partner Program enables qualified innovation hubs, applied-technology centres, training providers, universities, industry associations, and robotics organisations to deliver this experience locally using a standardized platform, curriculum, and delivery model.

## The learning model

The primary entry point is the **Robot Operator & Trainer** program. Participants move from understanding a business problem to operating a robot and teaching it by demonstration.

| Step | What the participant does | Business outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Assess** | Identify a realistic automation task and understand ROI and safety constraints. | A qualified use case rather than a vague automation idea. |
| **2. Operate** | Run missions, navigation and docking, use the operator UI, monitor the robot, and handle routine operation. | Internal capability to operate the platform independently. |
| **3. Show** | Demonstrate a task through guided teleoperation and capture demonstrations on the real training pipeline. | Domain knowledge becomes structured robot-training data. |
| **4. Correct** | Improve behaviour with corrective demonstrations where the robot gets the task wrong. | Faster iteration without traditional robot programming. |
| **5. Judge** | Evaluate task execution and decide whether the skill is good enough for the intended outcome. | A practical acceptance criterion tied to the real process. |
| **6. Plan deployment** | Translate the result into a site, safety, cost, and pilot plan. | A decision-ready roadmap for the next step. |

!!! tip "No coding required for the core path"
    Technical programs are available for teams that want to build, customize, or engineer the platform, but coding is not the entry requirement for the Operator & Trainer pathway.

## From training to real automation

Botshare Academy is designed as an **adoption channel, not education in isolation**.

<div class="oamr-legal">
  <strong>TRAIN → ASSESS → PILOT → DEPLOY → EXPAND</strong><br><br>
  A workshop or training cohort identifies a real process, tests whether it is suitable for automation, and creates the basis for a feasibility study or paid OpenAMRobot pilot. Successful pilots can progress to robot deployments, support, software, and additional use cases.
</div>

### Why this matters for partners

| Training business | Robotics business |
| --- | --- |
| Recurring professional-training revenue | Pipeline of qualified automation opportunities |
| Corporate cohorts and workshops | Feasibility studies and paid pilots |
| Certification and participant fees | Deployment, integration, and local support opportunities |
| Demo days and public programs | Long-term customer relationships around OpenAMRobot |

This model allows a local partner to become more than a classroom provider: it can evolve into an **Embodied AI competence and deployment hub** for its region.

## Delivery formats

Partners can be authorized for one or more formats depending on their capabilities.

| Format | Typical duration | Typical purpose |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| **Discovery Workshop** | 1 day | Hands-on introduction: operate the robot, navigate, and complete a first demonstration. |
| **Robot Operator & Trainer** | Multi-session / cohort | Full practical pathway from robot operation to training by demonstration and assessment. |
| **Corporate Enablement** | 4–8 weeks | Training cohort plus advisory around a selected automation task and deployment plan. |
| **Build Your Robot** | Program-based | Guided assembly, configuration, calibration, and verification of the OpenAMRobot DIY platform. |
| **Customize & Engineer** | Program-based | Technical depth covering selected software, firmware, electronics, and mechanical topics. |
| **Demo Day / Test-Before-Invest** | 1 day | Partner-hosted practical robotics experience for SME and innovation communities. |

!!! info "Start narrow, expand later"
    A partner does not need to deliver the full portfolio on day one. Authorization can begin with the Discovery Workshop and Robot Operator & Trainer program, then expand as instructors, facilities, and local demand mature.

## Partnership structure

The program uses a straightforward licensing model rather than requiring partners to build their own robotics curriculum and delivery infrastructure.

| Component | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Partner onboarding** | Technical setup, instructor preparation, delivery standards, and support for the first program. |
| **Train-the-Trainer certification** | At least one approved instructor for independent delivery of official programs. |
| **Annual partner license** | Commercial delivery rights, curriculum access, program updates, certification framework, and Certified Training Partner designation. |
| **Participant / certification fees** | Variable fees where applicable, scaling with actual delivery rather than a large fixed commitment. |
| **OpenAMRobot training platform** | Access to an approved robot/training configuration appropriate for the programs being offered. |
| **Pilot & deployment cooperation** | Separate commercial terms based on the partner's role in sales, engineering, integration, and local support. |

!!! note "Founding phase"
    Specific commercial terms are agreed individually while the program is being validated with the first partners. We are deliberately avoiding premature territorial exclusivity or rigid franchise structures at this stage.

## Quality standards

The value of the network depends on consistent technical and educational quality.

Certified partners are expected to:

- use approved instructors for official programs;
- follow the applicable Botshare Academy curriculum and practical assessment criteria;
- maintain a suitable OpenAMRobot training environment;
- follow robot safety and operating guidance;
- maintain instructor competence as the platform evolves;
- collect participant and customer feedback;
- clearly distinguish official Botshare Academy certification from independent courses built around the open-source project; and
- follow Botshare Academy brand and certification guidelines.

!!! warning "Open source and commercial certification are separate"
    OpenAMRobot remains an open-source project and can be used according to its applicable licenses. **Certified Training Partner status, Botshare Academy curriculum, commercial certification, and partner branding are separate commercial program elements.**
