# Botshare Academy Certified Training Partner Program

## Bring practical Embodied AI training to your market

**Become a Botshare Academy delivery partner and bring hands-on robotics and Embodied AI training to companies, entrepreneurs, and SME communities using OpenAMRobot.**

Botshare Academy is the professional training and adoption layer of the OpenAMRobot ecosystem. It is built for **domain experts — the people who understand the task but are not robotics engineers**.

The core idea is simple: instead of teaching every participant to become a robotics specialist, we teach them to **operate a robot, demonstrate a task, correct its behaviour, evaluate the result, and connect the skill to a real business outcome**.

> **Domain expertise — not coding — becomes the programming interface.**

The Certified Training Partner Program enables qualified innovation hubs, applied-technology centres, training providers, universities, industry associations, and robotics organisations to deliver this experience locally using a standardized platform, curriculum, and delivery model.

---

## What partners deliver

Partners can deliver approved Botshare Academy programs to their local companies and professional communities.

The primary entry point is the **Robot Operator & Trainer** program. Participants learn to:

1. **Assess** — identify a realistic automation task and understand ROI and safety constraints.
2. **Operate** — run missions, navigation and docking, use the operator interface, monitor the robot, and handle routine operation.
3. **Show** — demonstrate a task through guided teleoperation and capture demonstrations on the real training pipeline.
4. **Correct** — improve behaviour through corrective demonstrations where the robot gets the task wrong.
5. **Judge** — evaluate task execution and decide whether the skill is ready for the intended business outcome.
6. **Plan deployment** — translate the training result into a site, safety, cost, and pilot plan.

The core path requires **no coding**. Technical programs for teams that want to build, customize, or engineer the platform can be added separately.

## Built on OpenAMRobot

This is not a simulation-only robotics course.

Botshare Academy is built on **OpenAMRobot**, Botshare's open-source autonomous mobile robotics platform. Training uses the same ecosystem in simulation and on physical hardware.

The platform includes the open mechanics, electronics, firmware, ROS 2 software, navigation, docking, operator interfaces, simulation, teleoperation, and the developing Embodied AI training pipeline.

This gives partners a transparent and reproducible technology base rather than a closed educational demonstrator. Participants can start in simulation and progress to real robot operation, demonstration capture, evaluation, and deployment planning.

## What the partner receives

### Approved training programs

A structured curriculum, instructor guidance, practical exercises, participant materials, assessment criteria, and delivery formats based on the Botshare Academy methodology.

### Train-the-Trainer

Selected instructors are trained on OpenAMRobot, the course methodology, practical exercises, assessment process, and safe operation before delivering official programs independently.

### OpenAMRobot training environment

Partners use an approved OpenAMRobot setup and the associated simulation, software, repositories, and learning resources. The open-source foundation remains inspectable and reproducible.

### Commercial delivery rights

Certified partners may commercially deliver the Botshare Academy programs included in their agreement within the agreed market and scope.

### Certification framework

Approved programs can lead to practical Botshare Academy certification. For the core program, assessment is based on **live robot tasks rather than completion quizzes**: participants demonstrate that they can operate a mission and/or teach and evaluate a robot skill.

### Digital delivery infrastructure

The program is designed around scalable infrastructure: online learning and administration, public technical documentation, GitHub-based hands-on resources, assignments, and verification processes. Partner-specific implementation is defined during onboarding.

### Updates and partner support

Partners receive the applicable curriculum and program updates as OpenAMRobot and the Academy evolve, together with technical and delivery guidance defined by their partner tier.

### Co-branded local delivery

Workshops, demo days, and approved programs can be delivered with local partners and innovation organisations, subject to Botshare brand and program guidelines.

## From training to real automation

Botshare Academy is designed as an **adoption channel, not education in isolation**.

```text
TRAIN → ASSESS → PILOT → DEPLOY → EXPAND
```

A company begins with a hands-on workshop or training program. During the program, the team brings a real process from its operation and evaluates whether it is a realistic candidate for automation.

A qualified opportunity can then progress into a feasibility engagement or paid OpenAMRobot pilot. Successful pilots can progress to robot deployments, software/support services, and additional use cases.

For a partner, this creates two complementary opportunities:

- recurring revenue from professional training; and
- participation in a local pipeline of robotics assessment, pilot, and deployment opportunities.

For Botshare, the network creates local competence, demonstration capacity, customer access, and a growing base of real-world use cases around OpenAMRobot.

## Delivery formats

Partners can be authorized for one or more formats depending on their capabilities.

| Format | Typical purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Discovery Workshop** | One-day hands-on introduction: operate the robot, navigate, and complete a first demonstration. |
| **Robot Operator & Trainer** | Full practical pathway from robot operation to training by demonstration and assessment. |
| **Corporate Enablement** | Training cohort plus advisory around a selected automation task and deployment plan. |
| **Build Your Robot** | Guided assembly, configuration, calibration, and verification of the OpenAMRobot DIY platform. |
| **Customize & Engineer** | Technical program covering selected software, firmware, electronics, and mechanical topics. |
| **Demo Days / Test-Before-Invest** | Partner-hosted practical robotics sessions for SME and innovation communities. |

Not every partner needs to deliver every program. Authorization can start with the core workshop and expand as instructors and facilities mature.

## Partnership structure

The program is intended to use a straightforward licensing model rather than requiring partners to build their own robotics curriculum and delivery infrastructure.

A standard partnership may include:

**Partner onboarding**  
Initial technical setup, instructor preparation, delivery standards, and first-program support.

**Train-the-Trainer certification**  
At least one approved instructor is required for independent delivery of official programs.

**Annual partner license**  
Covers the agreed commercial delivery rights, curriculum, program updates, certification framework, and use of the Certified Training Partner designation while the partnership remains in good standing.

**Participant / certification fees**  
Where applicable, fees scale with actual program delivery rather than requiring a large fixed commitment from the partner.

**OpenAMRobot training platform**  
Partners delivering physical programs maintain access to an approved robot/training configuration appropriate for the programs they offer.

**Pilot and deployment cooperation**  
Commercial terms for customer opportunities that progress beyond training are defined separately according to the partner's role in sales, engineering, integration, and local support.

Specific commercial terms are agreed individually during the founding phase of the program.

## Quality standards

The value of the network depends on consistent technical and educational quality. Certified partners are therefore expected to:

- use approved instructors for official programs;
- follow the applicable Botshare Academy curriculum and practical assessment criteria;
- maintain a suitable OpenAMRobot training environment;
- follow robot safety and operating guidance;
- maintain appropriate instructor competence as the platform evolves;
- collect participant and customer feedback;
- clearly distinguish official Botshare Academy certification from independent courses built around the open-source project; and
- follow Botshare Academy brand and certification guidelines.

OpenAMRobot remains an open-source project. Organisations are free to use the open project according to its applicable licenses. **Certified Training Partner status, Botshare Academy curriculum, commercial certification, and associated partner branding are separate parts of the Botshare partner program.**
