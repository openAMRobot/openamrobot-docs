## The learning model

The core program is **Robot Operator & Trainer**. It follows a practical progression from business problem to deployment-ready decision.

| Stage | What the participant does | What the company gets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Assess** | Identify a realistic automation task; understand constraints, ROI and safety. | A qualified use case, not a vague automation idea. |
| **Operate** | Run missions, navigation and docking, use the operator interface, monitor the robot. | Internal capability to operate the platform. |
| **Show** | Demonstrate a task through guided teleoperation and capture training data. | Domain knowledge converted into robot-training data. |
| **Correct** | Add corrective demonstrations where the robot gets the task wrong. | Faster iteration without traditional robot programming. |
| **Judge** | Evaluate execution and decide whether performance is good enough. | A practical acceptance criterion tied to the real process. |
| **Plan** | Translate results into site, safety, cost and pilot requirements. | A concrete next-step deployment plan. |

!!! tip "No coding required for the core path"
    Technical programs are available for teams that want to build or customize the platform, but coding is not the entry requirement for the Robot Operator & Trainer pathway.

## From training to real automation

Botshare Academy is designed as an **adoption channel, not education in isolation**.

<div class="academy-process">
  <div class="academy-process__step"><strong>Train</strong><span>Give the team hands-on capability with the robot.</span></div>
  <div class="academy-process__step"><strong>Assess</strong><span>Bring one real process and determine whether it is suitable.</span></div>
  <div class="academy-process__step"><strong>Pilot</strong><span>Turn a qualified task into a scoped paid implementation.</span></div>
  <div class="academy-process__step"><strong>Deploy</strong><span>Move successful workflows toward real operating environments.</span></div>
  <div class="academy-process__step"><strong>Expand</strong><span>Add support, more use cases, software, or additional robot units.</span></div>
</div>

| Training business | Robotics business |
| --- | --- |
| Corporate workshops and recurring cohorts | Qualified automation pipeline |
| Participant and certification revenue | Feasibility studies and paid pilots |
| Demo days and regional programs | Integration and local-support opportunities |
| Instructor capability and community | Long-term customer relationships around OpenAMRobot |

A mature local partner can therefore become more than a classroom provider: it can develop into an **Embodied AI competence and deployment hub** for its region.

## Delivery formats

Partners can be authorized for one or more formats according to their capabilities.

| Format | Typical duration | Best for | Main outcome |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| **Discovery Workshop** | 1 day | Companies, entrepreneurs, SME groups | Operate the robot, understand the platform, complete a first demonstration. |
| **Robot Operator & Trainer** | Cohort / multi-session | Domain experts and operational teams | Full practical pathway from operation to teaching-by-demonstration. |
| **Corporate Enablement** | 4–8 weeks | Companies with a defined automation need | Training plus assessment and planning around a selected task. |
| **Build Your Robot** | Program-based | Technical teams, labs, educators | Assemble, configure, calibrate, and verify OpenAMRobot. |
| **Customize & Engineer** | Program-based | Engineering teams | Go deeper into selected software, firmware, electronics, and mechanics. |
| **Demo Day / Test-Before-Invest** | 1 day | Hubs, associations, regional programs | A practical robotics experience for multiple SME participants. |

!!! info "Start narrow"
    A partner does not need to deliver the full portfolio on day one. The recommended starting point is the Discovery Workshop and Robot Operator & Trainer pathway. Additional programs can be added as instructors, facilities, and local demand mature.

## Partnership model

The program uses a **licensing and certified-delivery model**, rather than requiring partners to reproduce Botshare's full business or operate as a traditional franchise from day one.

| Component | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Partner onboarding** | Technical setup, instructor preparation, delivery standards, and first-program support. |
| **Train-the-Trainer certification** | Creates approved instructors capable of independent delivery. |
| **Annual partner license** | Covers agreed commercial delivery rights, curriculum access, program updates, certification framework, and partner designation. |
| **Participant / certification fees** | Variable commercial component that can scale with actual delivery. |
| **OpenAMRobot training platform** | Approved robot and simulation environment appropriate to the programs offered. |
| **Pilot & deployment cooperation** | Separate commercial terms according to the partner's role in sales, engineering, integration, and support. |

!!! note "Founding phase"
    Specific commercial terms are agreed individually while the model is being validated with the first partners. We are deliberately avoiding premature territorial exclusivity and rigid franchise structures at this stage.
