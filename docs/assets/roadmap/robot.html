Snapshot 24 Oct 2026

Robot: OpenAMRobot 2.0 at P-03 rev19.7

What we are building
An open-source dual-arm mobile manipulator: our own mobile base with a DOLD Hexalift V4 lift and two OpenArm 2.0 arms, ROS 2 Jazzy software, released as a package a second site can build. The 2.0 target application is Use Case 1.
Where we are
The model below follows P-03 rev19.7, the register and the URDF on main and in PR #14. In Gazebo, base navigation works only with fixtures, docking has 0 of 5 successes, and arm planning works on mock hardware on a branch. Simulation at about 0.1 real-time factor; not safety evidence.
What is next
Fix the simulation on main (body filter, base command timeout, camera on base_camera_link, near-field consumers) and merge the combined model (PR #14) so the evidence below can be rerun on main.

    Basis and assumptions

    Design values from P-03 rev19.7, then E-01 rev1.4, then BOM Issue 7.10, cross-checked against the decisions register (decisions.yaml at 1c3f516). Dimensions not set by these documents come from the URDF on platform-sw main 8a7a5b1 and the combined model in openamr-upperbody-sw PR #14 (head e5cf613, draft). Floor Z = 0, base_link 98 mm above the floor, steel deck 294 mm, 2 mm top cover, 10 mm base plate (top 304 mm). Column axis x +0.060 m (bp000) or +0.110 m (bp050) from base_link; Hexalift V4 530 mm retracted, 350 mm stroke; arm mount 180 mm ahead of the column axis, y ±31 mm, 166 mm above the column top, so the shoulder runs 1000 to 1350 mm with stops L1000, L1175, L1350. ZED Mini lens 42 mm ahead of and 223 mm above the shoulder, pitch hp15 to hp35 (default hp30, the P-03 rev20.0 baseline since 28 Oct 2026, items 15, 17 and 18; the register still lists hp25, update pending). The view opens with two preview settings that no design source states as a default: base_plate bp000 (the PR #14 baseline) and the lift stop chosen automatically for the pick (simulation).

    Base sensors (P-03 rev19.7 item 19, decided): base_link 0.098 m above the floor; base_camera_link (0.387, 0, 0.046) with up-tilt bt05, bt10 (default, 10 deg up, the P-03 and register baseline) or bt15 drift 7; lidar_link (0.330, 0, 0.124) yaw pi on the MMP.07 bracket with a 17.5 mm printed spacer and no standoffs, scan plane 222.05 mm drift 6; imu_link (0.0544, 0, 0.1210) yaw -90 deg drift 5; ultrasonic us_* (±0.390, ±0.130, -0.0066), rear yaw pi drift 4; tof_front and tof_rear (±0.390, 0, -0.0066), rear yaw pi, pitched 10 deg down drift 2 drift 3. The model shows these design values. On platform-sw main (sensor_frames.yaml, matching the xacro): us_front 0.3906, us_rear -0.3886, tof ±0.399 pitched 20 deg; draft platform-sw #93 sets the ToF pitch to 10 deg and keeps x ±0.399 (implementation drift, OA-10 and OA-11).

    Base: chassis from CAD v0.2 (openamr-platform-hw at eaac115), 2.0 components from BOM 7.10, via openamr-platform-sw PR #94. Components are simplified envelopes; the near-field sensors follow the URDF frames on platform-sw main (ToF pitched 20 deg, drift 2) until #93 is merged. The battery cell-group envelope (eight EVE LF105 cells upright in one row, 314 x 140 x 210 mm; provisional, dimensions not released) stands on the rear deck with its front face 2 mm behind the centre bracket (project-lead decision of 25 Oct 2026); the BMS, power distribution, fuse and wiring are not drawn. Drawn at 35 % opacity, from openamr-platform-sw PR #95. The base E-stop (robot left, +y) and the RESET button (robot right, -y) sit in the front-panel holes of the release 001 buttons (P-03 rev20.0 item 24); both are placeholders from datasheet dimensions, from openamr-platform-sw PR #100 at 1939b82. The geometry is shared with the Exploded view page (model/).

    Drawn as placeholders: the base E-stop and the RESET button at the release 001 button holes of the front panel (openamr-platform-sw #100, draft), and on the rear face of the static lift profile, close to its upper edge, the second E-stop (the same placeholder as the base E-stop). The tablet (Getac ZX80) and the head light below it are not shown in this store scene until work package M-09 sets their positions (the arms pass through their placeholder positions); the exploded view shows them. Second E-stop and chest positions are placeholders until work package M-09 (P-03 rev20.0 item 24, layout update of 28 Oct 2026). Not released, drawn as illustrations: wireless charging receiver under the chassis, column lateral width (240 x 60 mm drawn), totes and riser. Track 0.400 m is a placeholder (DRIVE-TRACK open).

    The pick sequence, reach margins and store are an illustration, not measured behaviour. The dark-store scene is 3.0 scope; OpenAMRobot 2.0 is built for Use Case 1. Simulation results on this page come from Gazebo runs, not from this view.

    OpenArm 2.0 kinematics and meshes come from the official openarm_description URDF (upstream licence). Hexalift V4: 800 N push, 18 mm/s, 11.7 kg catalogue mass (P-03 item 16).
