Snapshot 24 Oct 2026

Progress: where we are, with evidence

What we are building
An open-source dual-arm mobile manipulator: our own mobile base with a DOLD Hexalift V4 lift and two OpenArm 2.0 arms, ROS 2 Jazzy software, released as a package a second site can build. The 2.0 target application is Use Case 1.
Where we are
Base navigation works in simulation only with fixtures; the safe command path, docking acceptance, 2.0 base firmware and the combined robot model are not on main; no physical acceptance is recorded. Since 19 Oct: command arbitration, the recovery behaviour tree and the STM32 CubeMX project are in review; only the Stop (software) label reached main. 111 PRs from 15 authors since 1 Oct (41 open).
What is next
Gate A and the electrical and safety release on 30 Oct, the I8 freeze and STM32 go/no-go on 6 Nov (P-02 rev19.2), the 13 Nov issue dues and Gate B regression 13 to 20 Nov, then v2.0.0-rc.1 on 20 Nov and physical integration from 23 Nov.

Physical validation: none recorded yet. The physical integration window is 23 Nov to 18 Dec 2026.
