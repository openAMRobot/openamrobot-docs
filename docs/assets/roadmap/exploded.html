    Exploded view: OpenAMRobot 2.0

    What this shows
    The 2.0 robot from the same model as the Robot page: assembled, exploded by subsystem, with the base opened so that its internal components stand above the deck, or as a cutaway with the cover and panels translucent. Every component is numbered, with its BOM Issue 7.11 row and a public source.
    Geometry
    Chassis parts are CAD-derived (CAD v0.2, openamr-platform-hw); base components are simplified envelopes shaped from their datasheet dimensions (the battery as eight LF105 cell boxes inside the cell-group envelope); the lift, plates, arm profile and head camera are primitives from decided dimensions; the arms are the OpenArm 2.0 meshes. Components without a documented position are listed but not drawn.
    Limits
    Visualization only, about ±2 cm. No fasteners, cables or wiring routes are shown; none are documented for 2.0. The battery is a cell-group envelope, provisional, dimensions not released. Positions in the opened base and the exploded views are for reading the parts, not assembly positions.

    Tour video

    The guided tour above, rendered offline from the same shared model: 1920 x 1080, 30 fps, H.264, no audio, 49.5 s; the vertical cut is 1080 x 1350. Download: 16:9 video (MP4), 4:5 vertical cut (MP4). Visualization only; not an assembly instruction.

    Snapshot 24 Oct 2026. Geometry: the shared roadmap model in model/ (model.js, base_glb.js, openarm.js; exploded and opened-base offsets, labels and the tour script in exploded.json; material classes in finish.json; renderer look and lighting in look.js), also used by the Robot page; the tour, the in-browser recording and the rendered videos draw that model and nothing else. Base: chassis from CAD v0.2 (openamr-platform-hw), 2.0 components as envelopes per BOM Issue 7.11 (25 Oct 2026) shaped from their datasheets, and the battery position per B-02 Battery Guide rev18.8, generated by tools/cad/step_to_description_meshes.py in openamr-platform-sw (one GLB node per part). Upper body: option B (openamr-upperbody-hw, P-03 rev19.0 items 16 to 19), lift column and plates as primitives until supplier CAD is available. Arms: OpenArm 2.0 meshes and kinematics from the upstream openarm_description URDF (upstream licence). Release 001 components are excluded.
