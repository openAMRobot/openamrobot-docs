Snapshot 19 Oct 2026

Hardware: BOM Issue 7.10 and E-01 rev1.3

What we are building
The 2.0 mobile base (two ZLTECH hub motors on a ZLAC8015D, 8S LiFePO4 battery, STM32H723ZG base controller, Jetson Orin NX) with a DOLD Hexalift V4 lift and two OpenArm 2.0 arms, behind a hardwired PNOZ s3 safety chain.
Where we are
The design baseline is documented in BOM Issue 7.10 and E-01 rev1.3. Open gates: BUY-32 (contactors), the PNOZ reset setting, the charging interface (WCM-500 wiring and receiver model) and the open shared-base differences. Nothing is built or accepted: the contactor order is blocked, the 2.0 firmware is not on main, and no physical test is recorded.
What is next
Close the GX11CAC datasheet checks (BUY-32), land the E-01 chain in the hardware repository, decide the rc.1 controller (STM32 go/no-go 6 Nov), then supervised bench tests from 23 Nov.

Physical validation: none recorded yet. The physical integration window is 23 Nov to 18 Dec 2026.

runtime: works in simulation with fixture runtime evidence (rounded) · physical: none recorded physical acceptance (box)

Open points (for the project lead)

  1. Is releasing RESET after the 5 s hold acceptable, given the PNOZ may close K1/K2 on release while the firmware stays LOCKED?
  2. How does the charge request reach the controller, and who decides?
  3. Does the under-2 s short-press rule still apply?

Verify before sending

  • Placeholder pins are free in the v0.0.1 firmware
  • ISO1212 output polarity on the bench
  • Idle and active levels of every input on the bench
  • Reset-response timeout value
  • Pull-up and series resistor values
  • Short-press and long-press thresholds

Principle: The PNOZ s3 and the contactors K1 and K2 are the safety function. The controller only reports and applies a secondary inhibit; nothing in this design may be relied on for safety.

AR-05: Planned closure of AR-05 for the Teensy path (commands held at zero across E-stop and RESET until an explicit operator release). The STM32 path needs the same behaviour; no design for it exists yet. See AR-05 and issue #17.

Open: The PNOZ reset setting (falling-edge dial position) stays open. The 200 ms /cmd_vel watchdog is an owner statement, not tested: the firmware documentation on main describes it, but no test record exists.

Connection layers

Solid lines: selected architecture at interface level. A line is a functional connection, not a conductor list.

Component and connection details

Hover, focus or select a component.

  • Interface-level view: buses and what connects to them. No pin tables.
  • No bus carries a safety function. The hardwired chain (two E-stops, PNOZ s3, K1/K2) is the only safety function; controller inputs are diagnostics.
  • An E-stop removes actuator power only (traction, both arms, lift and brake release); the Jetson, the 5 V rail and the regulated 24 V rail stay powered.
  • Charging: the charge relay NC contact opens the PNOZ supply, so actuators cannot be enabled while charging, and a deliberate RESET is needed after.
