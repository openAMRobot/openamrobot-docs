Mast and shoulder mounting

The OpenArm J1_A plates bolt straight onto the side T-slots of the MISUMI profile, so the official arm kinematics stay exact and the shoulder moves between four indexed positions in 50 mm steps. Installation height 1350 mm; mast top 1500 mm.

One M6 through-bolt in the index cross hole locates both J1_A plates at the selected height.

M5 bolts into 6-series T-nuts carry the arm loads, four per plate.

Dashed outlines show the plate at mast_1300, mast_1350 and mast_1450.

Arm and camera cables run in the rear slot zone and loop to each shoulder, long enough for one 50 mm step.

Cross holes: diameter 6.6 mm at 50 mm pitch, ordered as a MISUMI alteration. Every height is a configuration ID propagated to URDF, MoveIt, calibration and dataset metadata. At mast_1450 the plate overhangs the mast top by 11 mm; its upper bolts stay inside the profile.
