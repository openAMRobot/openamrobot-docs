# Entrepreneur path

For people building training, integration, hardware, pilot or support businesses around OpenAMRobot.

<div class="oamr-path"><span>Discover</span><b>→</b><span>Choose a model</span><b>→</b><span>Validate</span><b>→</b><span>Launch</span><b>→</b><span>Deliver</span><b>→</b><span>Support</span></div>

1. Review the [business models](../business/business-models.md).
2. Validate a concrete customer workflow, willingness to pay and delivery constraints.
3. Choose an offer whose claims match demonstrated platform capability.
4. Define acceptance, onboarding, support and escalation before launch.

**Finish when:** a specific customer problem, offer, delivery process and measurable pilot decision are documented.
