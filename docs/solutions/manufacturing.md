# Manufacturing

**For:** manufacturing SMEs, digitalization hubs and integrators evaluating mobile automation.

Candidate workflows include line-side delivery, internal inspection, material handling, machine-tending preparation and operator assistance.

## Qualification checklist

- A named process owner and budget owner
- Measurable labor, waiting, error or safety problem
- Defined material, route and hand-off
- Known site and safety constraints
- Acceptable human intervention level
- A pilot metric and commercial next step

**Relevant capabilities:** missions, schedules, maps, keep-out zones, docking, monitoring, integration APIs and future manipulation packages.
