# Facility and service operations

**For:** facility operators, service providers and local deployment partners.

Candidate workflows include recurring inspection, internal delivery, patrol, cleaning support and movement of supplies between named areas.

| Design area | Questions |
| --- | --- |
| Environment | Doors, lifts, people, reflective surfaces, floor transitions? |
| Operation | Who starts, monitors, pauses and recovers the mission? |
| Service | Who performs checks, updates and local repair? |
| Economics | Frequency, time saved, service level and avoided delay? |

Local service capacity is often as important as the robot itself. Include maintenance and escalation in the pilot design.
