# Entrepreneur track

Use this track to validate, launch and operate a business around the OpenAMRobot ecosystem.

## Planned route

1. Understand the platform, delivery layers and current capability boundary.
2. Select a service, training, integration, hardware or pilot model.
3. Validate a customer problem and define evidence for a paid engagement.
4. Scope, price, deliver and support a bounded offer.
5. Join the partner pathway and build repeatable local operations.

**Primary destination:** [Build a business](../business/index.md).
